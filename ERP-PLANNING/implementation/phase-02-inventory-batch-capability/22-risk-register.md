## Cross-cutting note

Every risk here is either a repeat-prone, already-documented repo pattern (P1, P6) this plan explicitly guards against by naming it, or a genuinely new judgment call this phase introduces and surfaces rather than silently resolving (P2, P3, P4, P5). None stem from the capability model itself being under-specified — consistent with the parent architecture's own risk register's cross-cutting note.

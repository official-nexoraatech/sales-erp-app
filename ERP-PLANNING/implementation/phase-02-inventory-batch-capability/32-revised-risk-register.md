## Cross-cutting note

Every risk here is either a repeat-prone, already-documented pattern this plan explicitly guards against by naming it (P1, P6, R1 via the phase split), a genuinely new judgment call surfaced rather than silently resolved (P2, P3, P4, P5, D1/D2/D4 in `26-decision-record.md`), or a newly-identified gap this revision's deeper investigation found and is recording rather than either fixing prematurely or silently dropping (R6, R7, R8). None stem from the capability model itself being under-specified.

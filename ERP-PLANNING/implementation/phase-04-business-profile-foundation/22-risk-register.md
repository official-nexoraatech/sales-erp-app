## Why this register is short

This phase's structurally low blast radius (`00-overview.md` §4) means most risk categories that dominated Phase 3's register (availability regression, entitlement mismatch, plan-tier drift) simply don't apply here — there is no enforcement mechanism this phase adds that could deny a legitimate request. The residual risks are all either naming/coordination (cheap to fix) or explicitly deferred to a correctly-later phase.

## Cross-cutting note

Every risk above traces to either (a) an already-known, already-documented gap this plan explicitly does not paper over, or (b) a process risk (drift, deprioritization, scope creep) rather than a design flaw in the target architecture itself. No risk here stems from the target Business Profile/Module/Capability model being under-specified — the model is deliberately thin and additive specifically to minimize the surface area for new risk.
